FAQ

Questions? We've got answers.

Do I need any experience to start?+

Not at all. Many of our courses are beginner-friendly and start from the very basics. Our skills quiz can also recommend the right starting point for you.

Can I learn at my own pace?+

Yes! All lessons are self-paced and available on any device. You can pause, resume, and revisit content whenever it suits your schedule.

Are the certificates recognized?+

Our certificates are shareable and verifiable, and many learners add them to LinkedIn and résumés. They demonstrate real, project-based skills to employers.

Can I cancel my subscription anytime?+

Absolutely. You can upgrade, downgrade, or cancel your plan at any time with no hidden fees or long-term contracts.

Do you offer support if I get stuck?+

Yes. Free learners have access to community forums, and Pro members can book live 1:1 mentor sessions for personalized guidance.
